Privacy Policy

Last updated: placeholder — set this to your actual launch date before publishing.

CanIStream provides free calculators and reference pages for streamers. This page explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the site.

The tools themselves collect nothing

Every calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Whatever you type in or select — your connection speed, hardware details, streaming setup — is processed on your device and is never sent to a server, stored, or seen by anyone. There's no account, no login, and no form submission happening behind the scenes.

A couple of tools use your browser's built-in APIs to guess at your hardware (like your graphics card) automatically. That detection also happens entirely on your device and is only used to pre-fill the form — it isn't transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

This site is supported in part by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some pages include affiliate links (for example, to gear like capture cards or external drives). If you click one and make a purchase, this site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps keep the tools free. Product recommendations are chosen because they're genuinely relevant to the tool you're using, not solely because of the commission.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand traffic — things like visit counts, which pages are popular, and which tools people click through to on Amazon. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and pages visited. This data helps figure out what's useful and worth building more of; it isn't used to identify you personally. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by declining analytics cookies if a cookie consent option is presented to you.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site grows — for example, if a new ad network or analytics tool is added. Check back here if you want the current version.
