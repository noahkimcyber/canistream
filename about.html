About CanIStream

CanIStream is a small set of free tools for streamers, built by someone who works in IT, has spent time editing video professionally, and streams on the side — the same person who kept getting asked to fix other people's OBS setups, and figured the answers could just live somewhere instead of being re-explained every time.

Every tool here runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter — your connection speed, your hardware, your streaming setup — gets sent to a server or stored anywhere. You can check the page source yourself if you want to verify that.

How this stays free

CanIStream is supported by ads and by affiliate links on some pages — if you click through to a product and buy it, this site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Recommendations aim to be genuinely useful, not just whatever pays the most. Full details are on the Privacy Policy page.

Accuracy

Platform bitrate limits, resolution caps, and similar numbers are checked against current platform and community guides, but streaming platforms do change these from time to time. If something here looks out of date, that's worth flagging directly with the platform rather than assuming this page is still current.

Questions, corrections, or a tool you wish existed? Reach out any time.
