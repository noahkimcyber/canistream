How this tool works (optional reading)
The hardware info above is a best guess from your browser, not a direct read of your PC — it's a real, working shortcut, but not perfect. Your upload speed and whether your CPU is busy can't be detected this way at all, since a browser page can't see your network test results or what else is running on your computer. A more complete version could measure your real upload speed, or connect straight to OBS itself to read your actual current settings — those are bigger builds than this quick version.
